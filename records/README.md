# Control Records

This directory documents control-record conventions.

Sensitive operational records should not be committed to a public repository merely to demonstrate that a control operated.

A control definition should identify where its authoritative records are retained and how their integrity, confidentiality, retention, and review requirements are satisfied.

Examples of future records include:

- governance review records;
- risk-review records;
- privileged-access reviews;
- incident exercise reports;
- business-continuity exercises;
- supplier reviews; and
- external assessment findings.

ISRAS-generated engineering assurance records remain governed by ISRAS and should be referenced rather than duplicated.
