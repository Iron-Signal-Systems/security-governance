## Identifiers

Real incidents use the next stable `ISS-INC-NNN`.

Exercises use `ISS-IR-EX-NNN`.

Identifiers shall not be reused.

## Required incident fields

A real incident record shall identify the incident ID, UTC detection time where
known, response status, severity, affected non-sensitive boundary, owner,
containment state, recovery state, notification state, related control/risk
records, and supported closure when applicable.

Sensitive identifiers, credentials, internal addresses, or protected technical
material shall not be published merely to populate this register.

## Maintenance

A real incident remains visible until closure. Closure does not delete its
identifier or history. Corrections shall preserve reconstructable history.
