## Rules

Normal approved exceptions expire within 90 days or sooner and do not renew
automatically.

High/Critical exception risk requires explicit approval and current risk
treatment.

This register cannot override authoritative ISRAS requirements.

A real emergency incident/recovery deviation shall be reviewed and recorded
within 24 hours once conditions permit.

Open risks, treatment actions, vulnerabilities, recovery limitations, supplier
limitations, future boundaries, and engineering actions are not exceptions
unless an actual intentional mandatory-control deviation exists.

## Maintenance

New exception records use the next stable `ISS-EXC-NNN` identifier.

Identifiers shall not be reused.
