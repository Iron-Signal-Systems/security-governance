# AGENTS.md

This repository defines Iron Signal Systems organizational security governance.

## Working rules

- Do not claim a control is implemented unless it is actually operating.
- Do not claim independent review when the same person performed and reviewed the work.
- Keep organizational governance separate from ISRAS engineering authority.
- Define and operate ISS controls before adding external certification or framework mappings.
- Do not place credentials, customer secrets, protected criminal-justice information, or other sensitive operational records in this repository.
- Preserve historical review and decision records rather than rewriting history.
- Make changes through reviewable commits with synchronized documentation and machine-readable control data where applicable.
