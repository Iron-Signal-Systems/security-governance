# Information Security Policy

## Objective

Iron Signal Systems protects information and systems according to their sensitivity, operational importance, contractual obligations, and security risk.

## Requirements

Iron Signal Systems shall:

- limit access to authorized users and services;
- use least privilege for administrative and production authority;
- protect authentication and cryptographic secrets;
- maintain an inventory of material information systems and suppliers;
- identify and track material security risks;
- govern software engineering through ISRAS where applicable;
- identify, assess, and remediate known vulnerabilities;
- detect, report, contain, investigate, and recover from security incidents;
- maintain business-continuity and recovery capabilities proportionate to operational risk;
- review material suppliers before entrusting them with sensitive information or operational dependencies;
- retain sufficient records to demonstrate that required controls operated; and
- review this policy after material changes and at least annually.

## Exceptions

Exceptions require explicit authorization through ISS-EXC-001 and must be bounded, reviewable, and time-limited where appropriate.
