# Access Control Policy

## Principles

- Access requires an authorized business or operational need.
- Privilege shall be limited to the minimum practical authority.
- Administrative access shall use individually attributable identities where supported.
- Shared credentials shall be avoided; when unavoidable, their use and custody shall be explicitly controlled.
- Authentication secrets shall not be committed to source repositories.
- Access shall be removed promptly when no longer required.
- Privileged access shall be reviewed at least quarterly.
- Production and release authority shall be separated where independence is required and qualified personnel exist.

## Provisioning

New access requires authorization by the applicable system or business owner.

## Removal

Access shall be removed after termination, role change, loss of business need, credential compromise, or other material authorization change.

## Review records

Privileged-access reviews shall identify the systems reviewed, accounts observed, authority retained or removed, reviewer, date, and unresolved findings.
