# Security Awareness Policy

## Purpose

People with material Iron Signal Systems access shall understand and acknowledge
the security responsibilities applicable to that access.

## Current organizational state

Iron Signal Systems is currently operated as a solo-developed project.

The current awareness boundary contains the sole project operator.

This policy does not imply employees, contractors, an HR department, a training
department, or a learning-management system.

## Requirements

Applicable security awareness shall be completed before or at material access
activation where practical and at least annually while that access continues.

Additional review is required when a material change, incident, finding, or
recurring error makes prior awareness materially incomplete.

Awareness shall address the security responsibilities actually relevant to the
person's access, including authentication and recovery protection, social
engineering, secret handling, least privilege, vulnerability response, incident
response, repository/source integrity, supplier dependence, information
handling, recovery limitations, and exception governance.

Completion requires affirmative acknowledgment.

AI or automation may assist preparation but does not constitute independent
human review.

## Future personnel

Before another person receives material ISS access, the awareness requirement
shall be incorporated into that access lifecycle.

No future employee or contractor process is represented as existing before it
actually exists.

## Records

Retain enough non-sensitive information to reconstruct the person/role boundary,
date, applicable topics, review authority, completion result, and next review.

Do not publish secrets merely to create a training record.
