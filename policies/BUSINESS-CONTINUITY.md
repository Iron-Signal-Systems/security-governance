# Business Continuity and Recovery Policy

## Purpose

Maintain recovery capabilities for critical ISS systems, repositories, services, credentials, and operational dependencies.

## Requirements

Critical assets shall have defined recovery requirements proportionate to business and customer impact.

Recovery plans should identify:

- dependency and data requirements;
- backup or reconstruction sources;
- restoration sequence;
- required credentials or authorities;
- recovery validation;
- responsible owner; and
- known limitations.

Material recovery procedures shall be exercised periodically. A backup shall not be considered a proven recovery capability merely because it exists.
