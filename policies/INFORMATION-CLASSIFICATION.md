# Information Classification Policy

## Initial classifications

- `PUBLIC` — approved for unrestricted public release.
- `INTERNAL` — routine ISS information not intended for public distribution.
- `CONFIDENTIAL` — sensitive business, customer, security, or personnel information requiring restricted access.
- `RESTRICTED` — highly sensitive information requiring explicit need-to-know and additional handling controls.

Credentials, private keys, authentication secrets, customer-controlled sensitive data, and protected investigative or criminal-justice information shall not be treated as PUBLIC merely because they appear in a development or troubleshooting context.
