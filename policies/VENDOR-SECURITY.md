# Supplier and Vendor Security Policy

## Purpose

Material suppliers shall be evaluated before ISS relies on them for sensitive information, source control, build or release infrastructure, customer operations, authentication, hosting, monitoring, or other critical dependencies.

## Review factors

Where applicable, review:

- service purpose and data handled;
- privilege and administrative access;
- geographic or hosting boundary;
- security and privacy commitments;
- incident-notification terms;
- continuity and recovery dependency;
- subcontractors or subprocessors;
- exit and data-return requirements; and
- available independent assurance.

The depth of review shall be proportional to risk.
