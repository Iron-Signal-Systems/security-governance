# Risk Management Policy

## Purpose

Define how Iron Signal Systems identifies, evaluates, treats, accepts, and
reviews material security and operational risks.

## Governing control

The operating risk-assessment control is:

`ISS-RSK-001 — Security Risk Assessment and Register`

## Required risk record

Each material risk shall include:

- stable risk identifier;
- description and affected boundary;
- cause or threat condition;
- potential impact;
- likelihood assessment;
- impact assessment;
- derived overall risk rating;
- accountable owner;
- treatment decision;
- related controls or treatment actions;
- target date where treatment is required;
- next review date; and
- current status.

A risk may initially be recorded as `IDENTIFIED` with assessment fields marked
`UNASSESSED`.

An identified risk shall not be represented as fully assessed until the required
fields have been reviewed and assigned.

## Likelihood

Likelihood uses:

- `1 — UNLIKELY`;
- `2 — POSSIBLE`; or
- `3 — LIKELY`.

## Impact

Impact uses:

- `1 — LIMITED`;
- `2 — MATERIAL`; or
- `3 — SEVERE`.

## Overall risk rating

Overall score is:

`likelihood × impact`

| Score | Rating |
|---:|---|
| 1–2 | LOW |
| 3–4 | MEDIUM |
| 6 | HIGH |
| 9 | CRITICAL |

If material uncertainty exists between two adjacent assessment levels and cannot
yet be resolved, use the higher level until the uncertainty is resolved.

## Treatment decisions

Permitted treatments are:

- `AVOID`;
- `REDUCE`;
- `TRANSFER`;
- `ACCEPT`; or
- `DISCONTINUE`.

High and Critical risks require either active treatment with an accountable
target date or explicit Governance Authority acceptance.

Risk acceptance must be explicit.

Acceptance does not override a mandatory organizational or engineering control.
Any deviation from a mandatory control requires the separately governed
exception process where applicable.

## Risk ownership

Risk ownership shall be assigned to the role accountable for ensuring the risk
remains governed.

Role-based ownership is permitted and preferred where it provides a durable
authority boundary.

## Review

The risk register shall be reviewed at least quarterly and after material:

- incidents;
- architecture or deployment changes;
- supplier changes;
- significant vulnerabilities or control deficiencies;
- asset-boundary changes;
- organizational or authority changes; or
- customer, contractual, or legal security changes.

Review records shall describe the actual assessment and shall not claim
independent review unless an independent qualified person performed it.
