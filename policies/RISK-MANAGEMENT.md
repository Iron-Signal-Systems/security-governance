# Risk Management Policy

## Purpose

Define how Iron Signal Systems identifies, evaluates, treats, accepts, and reviews security risks.

## Required risk record

Each material risk shall include:

- stable risk identifier;
- description and affected boundary;
- cause or threat condition;
- potential impact;
- likelihood assessment;
- impact assessment;
- overall risk assessment;
- accountable owner;
- treatment decision;
- related controls;
- target date where treatment is required;
- review date; and
- current status.

## Treatment decisions

Permitted treatments are avoid, reduce, transfer, accept, or discontinue the risk-producing activity.

Risk acceptance must be explicit. Acceptance does not override a mandatory control unless a separately governed exception authorizes the deviation.

## Review

The risk register shall be reviewed at least quarterly and after material incidents, architecture changes, supplier changes, significant vulnerabilities, or major business changes.
