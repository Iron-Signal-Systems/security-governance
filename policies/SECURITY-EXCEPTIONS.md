# Security Exception Policy

## Purpose

Intentional temporary deviations from mandatory Iron Signal Systems security
requirements shall be explicit, bounded, risk-informed, approved, time-limited,
and reconstructable.

## Fail-closed requirement

When a mandatory requirement applies and cannot be satisfied, the dependent
activity shall not proceed unless the governing requirement permits an exception
and an applicable approved exception is active.

No record means no exception approval.

## Authority

Security Authority evaluates the exception.

Governance Authority approves or rejects it.

In the current solo-project boundary, the same person may perform both
responsibilities. Such approval is self-approval and is not independent review.

## Duration

Normal approved exceptions expire within 90 days or sooner.

There is no automatic renewal.

Continued deviation requires a new review and approval decision.

## Risk

High or Critical exception risk requires explicit Governance Authority approval,
current risk treatment, compensating measures where reasonably available, a
treatment/closure plan, and fixed expiration.

An approved exception does not automatically accept or close risk.

## Engineering authority

Organizational exception authority does not override authoritative ISRAS
requirements.

## Emergency security action

Urgent containment or recovery may precede normal paperwork when delay would
materially increase harm.

An actual mandatory-control deviation created by the emergency action shall be
reviewed and recorded within 24 hours once conditions permit.

## Records

Exception records shall identify scope, requirement, rationale, risk,
compensating measures, authority, dates, status, and closure or renewal basis.

Secrets and sensitive exploit detail shall not be published merely to support an
exception record.
