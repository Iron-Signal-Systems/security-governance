# Iron Signal Systems Authority Register

## Purpose

This register identifies the governance and engineering authority assignments
currently operating under the Iron Signal Systems name.

Authority assignments describe responsibility. They do not imply employment,
officer status, incorporation, an LLC, or another separate legal entity.

## Current operating model

Iron Signal Systems is currently operated by one person.

Because only one person presently exercises these responsibilities, multiple
roles may be assigned to the same person.

Shared role assignment does not establish independence. A person shall not
represent review of their own work as independent review.

## Current assignments

| Role | Current assignment | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Governance Authority | Sole project operator | Organizational security governance, risk acceptance, organizational exceptions, governance review |
| Security Authority | Sole project operator | Security-policy operation, security risk, access governance, incidents, vulnerability governance |
| Engineering Authority | Sole project operator operating under ISRAS | Engineering decisions within the authority and constraints established by accepted ISRAS |
| System Owner | Sole project operator | Systems, services, repositories, and infrastructure operated under the Iron Signal Systems name |

## Governance Authority

The Governance Authority is the highest current authority for organizational
security governance under the Iron Signal Systems name.

The Governance Authority may:

- approve and revise organizational security policies;
- establish organizational controls;
- assign governance roles;
- accept organizational risk where permitted;
- authorize organizational exceptions where permitted;
- require corrective action;
- conduct governance reviews; and
- require independent external review when independence is necessary.

The Governance Authority shall not override an authoritative ISRAS engineering
requirement merely by exercising organizational authority.

Where an engineering requirement is governed by ISRAS, changes to that
requirement shall occur through the applicable ISRAS governance process.

## Security Authority

The Security Authority operates security controls assigned to that role.

Current responsibilities include:

- security risk management;
- identity and access governance;
- vulnerability governance;
- incident response;
- security awareness;
- security exceptions where authorized; and
- security-control review.

## Engineering Authority

Engineering Authority remains subject to the applicable accepted ISRAS
authority boundary.

Organizational governance may establish objectives, risk constraints, and
security requirements for engineering work, but it shall not silently replace
or bypass an authoritative ISRAS engineering control.

## Independence

The present operating model does not provide internal personnel independence.

A self-review may be valid as an internal governance activity when independent
review is not required, but it shall be identified as self-review.

When independent review is required, it must be performed by a qualified person
who did not perform the work being independently assessed.

Automation, artificial-intelligence systems, alternate accounts, alternate
signing keys, or separate sessions do not constitute independent human review.

## Change of authority

This register shall be reviewed and updated when:

- another person receives governance, security, engineering, or system authority;
- Iron Signal Systems forms or becomes part of a separate legal entity;
- personnel or organizational structure changes materially;
- independent internal roles become possible; or
- an existing authority assignment is revoked or materially changed.

Historical authority assignments shall remain reconstructable through signed
repository history.
